# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our commitment

Everyone is welcome to use, discuss and contribute to ASCII Local, whatever their experience, background or identity. We want this project to be a friendly and respectful place.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the code, not about the person.
- Respect different opinions and ways of working.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or images.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any behaviour that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report any problem to the maintainer at boris.fr.67@gmail.com. Every report will be read and handled privately.

Depending on the situation, the maintainer may give a warning, edit or delete comments, or temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project.
